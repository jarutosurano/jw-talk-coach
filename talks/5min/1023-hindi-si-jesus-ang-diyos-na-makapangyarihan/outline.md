## Counsel Point: th aralin 7 — Tumpak at Nakakakumbinsi

**KUNG ANO ANG GAGAWIN:** Gumamit ng mapagkakatiwalaang mga ebidensiya para matulungan ang mga tagapakinig na makabuo ng tamang konklusyon.

**How the script applies it:**
- **Mapagkakatiwalaang reperensiya** — only the Bible; both outline texts are read aloud.
- **Tamang paraan / konteksto** — Juan 14:28 framed as the night before Jesus died; 1 Cor 11:3 framed as written after Jesus was back in heaven (answers "mas mababa lang siya noong tao pa").
- **Hindi sobra** — the theme is stated exactly ("Diyos na Makapangyarihan-sa-Lahat"), not "hindi Diyos si Jesus." The headship text is explained without belittling Jesus (headship ≠ less value).
- **Mangatuwiran batay sa ebidensiya** — after each text, a tactful question lets the audience reach the conclusion themselves.
