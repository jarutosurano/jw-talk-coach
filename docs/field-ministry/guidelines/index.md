# Field Ministry Service Guidelines

Mga praktikal na gabay para sa meetings for field service — mula sa mga publikasyon ng organisasyon.

> Layunin: Gamitin ang bawat item bilang checklist o practice guide para sa mga conductor at publisher.

---

## A. Midweek Meeting — Meetings for Field Service

Ang mga guidelines na ito ay para sa meetings for field service na ginaganap bago lumabas sa ministeryo.

---

### 1. Duration at Format

| Item | Guideline | Practice Notes |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Duration | **5-7 minuto** (simula April 2015) | Gamitin ang timer sa phone |
| Kapag kasunod ng ibang meeting | Mas maikli pa | Naka-receive na ng scriptural content ang mga dumalo |
| Nagsisimula | On time | Huwag maghintay ng late comers |
| Nagtatapos sa | Prayer + territory assignment | Lahat may alam na kung saan pupunta |

!!! tip "Ikapit"
    Bago mag-meeting, i-practice ang 5-minute timer. Kung lumampas ka, i-trim ang discussion — hindi ang territory assignment.

---

### 2. Conductor Preparation

| Item | Guideline | Practice Notes |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Territory | Handa na bago mag-meeting | Huwag mag-organize ng territory habang nag-uusap pa |
| Content | Practical application para sa ministry ngayong araw | "Ano ang makakatulong sa kanila ngayong araw?" |
| Assignment | Matanggap nang maaga | Huwag maghanda ng last minute |
| Goal | Encourage at equip | Hindi lecture — encouragement |

!!! quote "Principle"
    "The conductor should think about what will encourage and equip those going out in the ministry that day."

---

### 3. Mga Suggested Discussion Topics

Piliin ang isa o dalawa na relevant sa group mo ngayong araw:

- [ ] Sample conversations mula sa *Meeting Workbook*
- [ ] Current events o news reports para mag-open ng conversation
- [ ] Paano mag-respond sa common objections
- [ ] Engaging with atheists, evolutionists, non-English speakers
- [ ] Paggamit ng jw.org, JW Library app, o Bible tools
- [ ] Resources mula sa Teaching Toolbox
- [ ] Specific ministry approaches:
    - [ ] Telephone witnessing
    - [ ] Letter writing
    - [ ] Public witnessing
    - [ ] Return visits
    - [ ] Bible studies
- [ ] Safety reminders, flexibility, courtesy, positive attitude
- [ ] Content mula sa *Apply Yourself to Reading and Teaching* brochure
- [ ] Encouraging field service experiences
- [ ] Relevant na scripture

!!! tip "Ikapit"
    Sa Saturday morning meeting — "it may be practical simply to consider what to say." Huwag mag-overthink. Simple lang: Ano ang sasabihin natin sa unang bahay?

---

### 4. Publisher Preparation

| Item | Guideline | Practice Notes |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Paghahanda | Sa family worship kung posible | I-practice ang presentation kasama ng pamilya |
| Pagdating | Ilang minuto bago mag-start | Huwag late — nakaka-disrupt |
| Materials | Kunin bago mag-meeting | Bible, bag, territory slip |
| Pakikinig | Makinig sa instructions ng conductor | Alamin ang assignment bago umalis |

---

### 5. Building Friendships sa Field Service Group

Ang field service group ay hindi lang organizational arrangement — ito ay pagkakataon para magkaroon ng malalim na samahan.

**Mga Practical Steps:**

- [ ] Makipagtrabaho sa iba't ibang miyembro ng group — huwag laging iisa ang kasama
- [ ] Mag-invite ng group member sa meal o family gathering
- [ ] Mag-offer na mag-host ng meeting sa bahay mo — hindi importante ang laki ng bahay
- [ ] I-take advantage ang shepherding visits ng group overseer
- [ ] Makipag-usap sa service overseer kapag bumisita siya sa group mo monthly

!!! quote "Principle"
    "Working alongside others in ministry 'side by side' can create meaningful bonds."

---

### 6. Attendance at Participation

| Item | Guideline | Practice Notes |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Pioneers | Mag-attend ng kahit ilang meetings per week | Example at encouragement sa iba |
| Experienced publishers | Mentor ng mga bago | Magpakita ng patience at encouragement |
| Territory coverage | Thorough at organized | Sundin ang biblical example — Luke 10 |
| Reassignment | Flexible | Okay lang mag-switch ng kasama paminsan-minsan |

---

### 7. Organizational Approaches

Depende sa local circumstances ng congregation:

=== "Separate Group Meetings"
    **Pros:**

    - Mas madaling travel papunta sa territory
    - Mas mabilis mag-organize
    - Mas close ang oversight ng group overseer

    **Best for:** Congregations na may malawak na territory

=== "Combined Meetings"
    **Pros:**

    - Mas maraming kasama
    - Mas madaling mag-assign ng partners
    - Good for smaller congregations

    **Best for:** Congregations na kaunti ang publisher o maliit ang territory

## B. CBS-Related — A Center for Field Ministry

Ang guidelines na ito ay originally para sa congregation book study arrangement, pero applicable pa rin ang mga principles.

---

### 8. Book Study Location as Field Ministry Center

| Item | Guideline | Practice Notes |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Purpose | "Serve as a meeting place for field service" | Fixed time + fixed location = consistency |
| Schedule | Accommodate most publishers | Tanungin ang group kung anong oras ang best |
| Consistency | Regular scheduling | "Publishers benefit from knowing there will be territory available and others to work with" |

---

### 9. Conductor Responsibilities (CBS Context)

- [ ] Organize the activity of the group
- [ ] Work directly with publishers in the field
- [ ] Prepare meetings that offer "Scriptural and practical instruction"
- [ ] Arrange for experienced publishers to mentor others
- [ ] Maintain "a positive and balanced view"
- [ ] Offer "commendation as progress is made"

!!! quote "Principle"
    "Mutual encouragement among group members to become 'effective in preaching the good news and making disciples' while supporting one another through shared ministry work."
